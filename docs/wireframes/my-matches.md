## Notes

All scholarship examples are fictional placeholders.

Matches are based on the selected profile's state,
grade level, and fields of interest.

National scholarships may also match eligible students.

Results are sorted by application deadline.

Students access their own profiles.

Parents can select among their children's profiles.

The functional My Matches page will be implemented in Iteration 8.
